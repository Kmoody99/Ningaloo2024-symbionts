# Thermal-history-and-the-condition-of-symbionts-lost-by-heat-stressed-corals-from-Ningaloo-Reef-Aus
Supplementary and raw data from two experiments examining in hospite and ex hospite symbiont condition, bleaching, Fv/Fm and necrosis at two thermally contrasting sites on the Ningaloo Reef World Heritage Area, Western Australia.
